Delete Your Account

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Nexgig account and the data associated with it. Nexgig ("Nexgig", "we", "us", or "our") is a booking and roster management platform that connects venue managers with artists (DJs and live performers).

Delete your account from the app

You can permanently delete your account at any time, directly inside the Nexgig app:

  1. Open the Nexgig app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in to the app, email us from the address on your account at admin@nexgigapp.com with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify your request and delete your account, normally within 7 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove your personal data, including:

  • Your login and authentication details;
  • Your profile — name, stage/legal name, email address, phone number, chosen avatar, bio, location, nationality, genres, instruments, and links;
  • For venue managers: your venues and their details;
  • Your availability, roster connections, notifications, and push-notification token.

What data may be kept

Some shared records are retained in de-identified form so the accounts you interacted with keep accurate histories. Specifically, past bookings and invoices may be kept as a business and financial record for the other party (the venue or artist you dealt with), with your personal link to them removed. Any residual copies in our encrypted system backups are purged within 30 days. We may also retain limited information where required to comply with legal obligations.

Questions

For anything related to your data or this process, contact us at admin@nexgigapp.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
